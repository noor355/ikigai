# AI Coach Test Scenarios

Use these scenarios to test the AI's ability to cross-reference multiple journal entries and provide specific career/salary advice.

## Scenario A: The Tech-Creative Hybrid
**Goal**: Test if the AI recognizes the intersection of high-level coding and artistic passion.

1.  **Journal Entry 1**: "Spent today mastering React and Three.js. I love how code can create 3D worlds, but I feel like I'm missing the 'soul' of traditional art."
2.  **Journal Entry 2**: "Visited a gallery today. The abstract paintings really spoke to me. I want my digital work to feel as textured and emotional as those canvas pieces."
3.  **Journal Entry 3**: "Built a small algorithm that generates color palettes based on my mood logs. It's a bridge between my logic and my emotions."
**AI Prompt**: "I've been journaling about React and abstract art. What career combines my technical skills with my need for emotional expression, and what do they pay?"

---

## Scenario B: The Healthcare-Tech Optimizer
**Goal**: Test if the AI can identify niche roles (Health Informatics/Product) from soft skills and domain interest.

1.  **Journal Entry 1**: "Volunteered at the clinic today. The patient data system is so slow and clunky. It's frustrating to see doctors waste time on bad UI."
2.  **Journal Entry 2**: "Reading up on data privacy and HIPAA. It's complex, but I actually enjoy the challenge of making systems secure but accessible."
3.  **Journal Entry 3**: "Helped my grandmother use a health-tracking app. She couldn't understand the graphs. We need better ways to explain medical data to people."
**AI Prompt**: "Looking at my recent entries about clinics and data privacy, what specific career should I pursue to fix these systems, and what is the salary range?"

---

## Scenario C: The Sustainability-Business Strategist
**Goal**: Test if the AI can connect passion for the environment with professional growth and financial viability.

1.  **Journal Entry 1**: "Started a community garden project. Managing the budget and the volunteers is exhausting but so rewarding."
2.  **Journal Entry 2**: "Was shocked to see how much waste the local grocery store produces. There must be a more efficient way to handle supply chains."
3.  **Journal Entry 3**: "Completed a certification in Supply Chain Management. I want to use this to make businesses more 'circular' and less wasteful."
**AI Prompt**: "Based on my garden project and my supply chain cert, what high-impact careers fit me, and how much can I earn doing it?"
